# Basis and limits

- WHO, *Doing What Matters in Times of Stress*, published 29 April 2020:
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927
- WHO and UNICEF, *Foundational helping skills training manual*, published 10 July 2025 with an
  English-artifact corrigendum dated 25 August 2025:
  https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240105935
- Western Australia Centre for Clinical Interventions, *Self Compassion*, page last updated
  5 September 2019:
  https://www.cci.health.wa.gov.au/Resources/Looking-After-Yourself/Self-Compassion

This is an original, narrow synthesis of noticing harsh self-judgment, validating shame or distress
without trivializing it, exploring barriers to self-kindness, and responding without escalating
self-attack. The sources do not establish that criticism is generally old, protective, or once
useful; the skill asks about those possibilities without assuming them. Its sequencing and
trauma-history stop rules are conservative safeguards, not causal claims. The materials do not
validate AI delivery, and no workbook or exercise wording is copied.
