# Basis and limits

- NHS Every Mind Matters, *Thought record*, current page verified 4 August 2026:
  https://www.nhs.uk/every-mind-matters/mental-wellbeing-tips/self-help-cbt-techniques/thought-record/
- NICE NG222, *Depression in adults: treatment and management*, published 29 June 2022 and last
  reviewed 30 January 2026:
  https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng222/chapter/Recommendations

The skill independently abstracts anchoring one thought to a concrete situation, examining evidence
for and against it, considering other accounts, and preserving links among thoughts, feelings, and
behavior. Unlike the NHS exercise, it does not supply or require a preferred alternative thought.
The sources are self-help or depression-specific and do not justify treating real danger, loss, or
injustice as a distortion or validate an AI intervention. NHS and NICE reuse constraints apply; no
worksheet or recommendation wording is copied.
